v6.3.1 denominator ordering audit

Observed v6.3.1 order in ep_disable:
  dwc2_r1_note_disable_candidate(...)
  dwc2_r1_stop_enter(...)
  dwc2_hsotg_ep_stop_xfr(...)

But note_disable_candidate reads r1->stop_causal, which stop_enter establishes.
Therefore the current stop's cause was not available to denominator accounting.

v6.3.2 required order:
  dwc2_r1_stop_enter(...)
  dwc2_r1_note_disable_candidate(...)
  dwc2_hsotg_ep_stop_xfr(...)

Same correction is applied to active ep_dequeue for one invariant across both
Bulk-OUT stop families.

Semantic consequence: candidate counters change meaning, therefore dump version
must change 8 -> 9 even though header/record sizes stay 112/80.
